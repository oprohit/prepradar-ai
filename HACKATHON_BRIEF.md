## Problem Statement

Colleges and universities often lack a centralized system to identify, report, and respond quickly to safety incidents such as medical emergencies, accidents, harassment, fire hazards, suspicious activities, and infrastructure problems.

Currently, students and staff may have to contact different departments through phone calls or messages, causing delays in emergency response and making it difficult for administrators to track incidents.

Develop a software platform that uses AI to provide real-time campus safety monitoring and emergency response.
